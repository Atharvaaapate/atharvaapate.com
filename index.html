About

Atharva Apate is an entrepreneur, tech builder, and founder driven by a passion for media and digital innovation. Fascinated by the power of technology from a young age, Atharva focuses on creating impactful digital systems and content agency models. As the founder of TRTL Media, he aims to bridge technology, marketing, and modern branding to empower businesses and creators worldwide.

His curiosity quickly expanded toward entrepreneurship and digital business models.
